FAQ

Questions before you send the job

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com with the site address, jurisdiction, due date, size, install yes or no, and whether you have artwork. Phone is (425) 405-0118 if the shop line is free.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com Contact

How fast can you print and install?

Temporary boards same day when we have the file and the truck is in range. Permanent boards are usually 2–3 days. Multi-site or permit-tied jobs follow the date you give us.

What do you need for a quote?

Address and jurisdiction (city or county), due date, size, install yes or no, and artwork vs. typeset from your text. A photo of the last board or the permit sheet helps. Site photos are optional.

Do you install?

Yes, shop print plus truck install in Western Washington. One-off jobs about 90–120 minutes from Lynnwood. Multi-site work anywhere in Washington. Install hardware (posts, bungees, rope) is quoted with the job, not baked into a banner-only price.

What materials do you print?

Coroplast, MDO, corrugated plastic, mesh, vinyl (including textured), lamination, and scrim banner. Boards in the ground are the main job: 4×4 through 4×8 print, install, and remove.

How much are banners?

Public menu is $15 per linear foot. Short side max 54 inches. 4×8 and 54″×8 are both $120. Hem is taped, grommets included. No pole pockets. Install is extra.

How much are truck door logos?

A pair: 12×24 $99, 18×36 $159, 24×48 $249. Pickup is free. Shipping and shop install are extra. Rectangle or contour-cut. We do not sell full vehicle wraps.

Boat letters?

3″ registration pair $99, 4″ pair $129, custom name from $99. Any font and color, rounded edges. Local install.

Where do you work?

Lynnwood shop, jobs across Snohomish and King and the rest of Western Washington. City pages live under Service area. Seattle jobs are accepted; we do not treat Seattle as a target city.

Can you match a county or city posting spec?

Yes for land-use and public notice. Send the packet or the last posted board. We pull from the county or city sheet, not a generic template.

How do I start?

Email the list to Sales@BigBassSigns.com. Use the contact page if that is easier. Call if you are standing on the site and need a same-day board.
