How fast can you print and install?

Temp boards as soon as same day when the file is clean. Permanent boards in two to three days. Banners and yard signs as soon as same day on a clean file before noon.

What do you need for a quote?

Site address, jurisdiction, due date, size or “match the packet,” install yes or no, and whether you have artwork. Land-use and public notice: attach the PDF. That is the whole packet.

Do you install?

Yes. Print, post, and remove. One-off installs sit about 90–120 minutes from Lynnwood. Multi-site work goes anywhere in Washington. Vehicle on-site install is $99 for up to three decals in the normal service radius.

What materials do you print?

Coroplast, MDO, corrugated plastic, vinyl, textured vinyl, mesh, scrim, and lamination. Print width up to 54 inches. Public banners are the 4-ft line.

How much are banners?

$15 per linear foot on a 4-ft banner. 4×8 is $120. 4×10 is $150. 4×12 is $180. 4×20 is $300. Print, hem, and grommets included. Wider than 4 ft is a quote.

How much are truck door logos?

Driver and passenger kits: 12×24″ $99, 18×36″ $159, 24×48″ $249. Ship, pickup, or on-site install +$99 for up to three decals in the normal service radius.

Boat letters?

3-inch registration pair $99. 4-inch pair $129. Custom names from $99. Any font or color with rounded edges.

Where do you work?

The shop is in Lynnwood. Western Washington install — Lynnwood, Everett, Redmond, Sammamish, Woodinville, and the rest of the service area. One-off installs about 90–120 minutes out. Multi-site work anywhere in Washington.

Can you match a county or city posting spec?

Yes. Forward the notice or the completeness letter. We match current city and county posting specs so the board matches what is required now.

How do I start?

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the packet or the logo. Call (425) 405-0118 if the shop line is free. Or use the contact page.
