Common questions

What is a recharge?

A recharge is new panels on a plastic board, or a new vinyl wrap on a wood board. Bring the frame back. You do not buy another one.

Can I order a new A-frame?

Yes. Plastic comes with replaceable printed panels. Wood is re-wrapped with vinyl. Small, medium, and large.

How much are A-frame signs?

Plastic is $199, $279, or $379. Wood is $179, $249, or $349. New plastic panels are $59, $79, or $99. A wood re-wrap is $49, $69, or $89.

Who are these sidewalk boards for?

Local businesses, restaurants, food trucks, salons, and shops in Lynnwood and Snohomish County. A sidewalk menu, daily special, or open sign is the usual job.

Which counties do you serve?

Snohomish County and King County. The signs are printed in Lynnwood.
