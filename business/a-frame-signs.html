Common questions

What is an A-frame makeover?

A makeover is new printed panels on a frame you already have. It is the usual way to change a seasonal menu, a drink list, or a weekend special without buying another frame.

Can I order a new A-frame?

Yes. A new A-frame is the frame plus the printed panels. A makeover is panels only, for a frame you already have.

Who are these sidewalk boards for?

Cafes, restaurants, food trucks, farm stands, retail, and events. Goat & Seed in Everett uses breakfast and coffee boards, and menu and drink boards.

Which counties do you serve?

Snohomish County and King County. The signs are printed in Lynnwood.
