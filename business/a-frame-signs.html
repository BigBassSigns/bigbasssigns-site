Common questions

What is an A-frame refresh?

A refresh is new printed panels on a frame you already have. It is the usual way to change a seasonal menu, a drink list, or a weekend special without buying another frame.

Can I order a new A-frame?

Yes. A new A-frame is the frame plus the printed panels. A refresh is panels only, for a frame you already have.

How much are A-frame signs?

Refresh from $150, brand new from $325.
