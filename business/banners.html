Scrim and mesh banners

We print banners up to 54 inches on the short side, any reasonable length. Scrim vinyl is the default for most outdoor and event work. Mesh banners are the construction and fence product — wind passes through so the face stays on the chain link instead of turning into a sail.

Every banner includes a strongly taped hem and intermediate grommets in the three main sizes. Pick silver or gold hardware. Hanging the banner, plus bungees, rope, or other install materials, is extra.

Yard signs

Coroplast yard signs and riders for real estate, short-run business, and events. Step stakes available. Send the size, the count, and artwork — or a logo plus three lines and we set the type.

Simple yard-sign runs can go the same day when the file is clean before noon. Pickup in Lynnwood or we drop them with a site install.
