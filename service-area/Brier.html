Brier is a small Snohomish city tucked between Lynnwood, Mountlake Terrace, and Lake Forest Park. Most notices here are City of Brier. If the parcel sits outside the city, the header is Snohomish County.

Send the notice PDF and the due date. We print in Lynnwood and post along 228th and the side streets with the other south-end jobs. Start with the land use sign spec in the packet.

Common questions

Who is the jurisdiction on a Brier land use sign?

City of Brier when the site is inside the city. Snohomish County when it is not. The packet says which.

What should I send before you print?

The notice PDF, the site address, and the date it has to be up. Add install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Is Brier close enough for a same-day post?

Yes for a temp board when the file is clean. Brier is a few minutes from the Lynnwood shop. Permanent boards take two to three days.
