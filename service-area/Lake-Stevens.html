Lake Stevens

Leasing signs and public notice boards for Lake Stevens.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Lake Stevens sits northeast of the shop, past Everett, on the shore of the lake and up SR 9. Frontier Village is at SR 9 and SR 204. 20th Street SE is the other commercial stretch on that highway. Downtown is a smaller stop on the north side of the lake. The drive from Lynnwood is about 15 miles, across the US 2 trestle and out SR 204.

A lease at Frontier Village or along 20th is a panel at the suite, and window lettering when the glass is part of the same order. Downtown does not use that shopping-center art. Send the street so the file matches the stop. A yard sign is the right piece for a house or a small lot, not for a retail bay.

The notice header is City of Lake Stevens only when the parcel is inside the city. Annexation did not take every lot around the lake, so an address that sounds like Lake Stevens can still be Snohomish County. Everett, Monroe, and the City of Snohomish are different letters. Read the notice and send it with the due date. A construction site sign for the same job can go on that trip.

Common questions

Is every Lake Stevens address a City of Lake Stevens notice?

No. The city limits do not cover every lot around the lake. If the letter says Snohomish County, the board is the county notice. Everett and Monroe headers do not go on a Lake Stevens parcel.

Where do leasing signs and storefronts go in Lake Stevens?

Most retail leasing is at Frontier Village and along 20th Street SE on SR 9. Downtown by the lake is a separate stop. Email the suite address with the file.

How fast can a Lake Stevens board be posted?

A clean temp board can go out the same day. Lake Stevens is about 15 miles from the Lynnwood shop, across the US 2 trestle. Permanent boards take two to three days.
