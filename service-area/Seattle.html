The shop is in Lynnwood. We do not advertise as a Seattle shop. When a packet names the City of Seattle, that board gets a Seattle header, printed in Lynnwood and posted from Lynnwood. Shoreline, just north of the city limit, is a different city. Unincorporated King County is a different board.

Central Seattle is about fifteen miles south of the shop. The closer posts are the north end, where Seattle starts along N 145th Street on the far side of Shoreline: Lake City, Northgate, Bitter Lake, Greenwood, and Aurora Avenue N inside the city. Ballard is a longer loop. A downtown, West Seattle, or Duwamish address is farther still, so the due date belongs in the first email.

Send the notice PDF, the site address, and install or board-only. If the parcel is outside Seattle, say which city or whether it is King County before we print. The land use sign spec covers the notice board. A project board uses the construction sign spec.

Common questions

Do you advertise as a Seattle shop?

No. The shop is in Lynnwood. We print a City of Seattle header when that is the letter, and we post it from Lynnwood. Shoreline and King County are different boards.

Which Seattle addresses are the closer posts?

The north end, just south of Shoreline along N 145th Street: Lake City, Northgate, Bitter Lake, Greenwood, and Aurora Avenue N inside the city. Downtown, West Seattle, and the Duwamish are a longer drive. Email the address and the due date to Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

What do you need before you print a Seattle board?

The notice PDF, the site address, the due date, and install or board-only. If the parcel is outside Seattle, name the city or say King County so the header is right.
