Island County

Land use signs and construction signs for Island County.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote (425) 405-0118

Island County covers Whidbey Island and Camano Island. The county seat is Coupeville. A city or town letter and an Island County letter are different boards. Langley, Coupeville, and Oak Harbor each use their own header. Camano Island and the rest of unincorporated Island County follow the county letter.

The shop is in Lynnwood, in Snohomish County. Whidbey jobs leave from the Mukilteo ferry. Camano jobs leave by road through Stanwood. Email the address, the jurisdiction on the letter, and the due date. The board is printed in Lynnwood.

Common questions

How do I know if the board is a city sign or Island County?

The letter in the packet names the jurisdiction. City of Langley, Town of Coupeville, City of Oak Harbor, and Island County are not the same header. Email the letter with the PDF.

Where are Island County signs printed?

In the Lynnwood shop. Whidbey posting goes by the Mukilteo ferry. Camano posting goes by road through Stanwood.

Which Island County places have their own page?

Langley. Coupeville and Oak Harbor are printed from this same Lynnwood shop. Unincorporated Island County, including Camano Island, uses the county letter.
