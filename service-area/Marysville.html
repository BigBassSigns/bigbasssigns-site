Marysville is the I-5 city north of Everett, about 19 miles from Lynnwood. State Avenue is the old commercial spine through downtown. Fourth Street runs from that avenue to the freeway and west toward Ebey Slough. Smokey Point, at the north end, straddles the Arlington line. The Tulalip Reservation is west of I-5 and is not the city.

A leasing panel or window lettering on State Avenue is a City of Marysville job when the address is inside the city. Name the city in the email for a Smokey Point suite, because the Arlington side of that area is a different letter. A site on the reservation is not a City of Marysville file either. A house listing uses a yard sign.

Construction notices follow the same line. City of Marysville, Snohomish County, and City of Arlington are three headers. We print the one in the packet, and a project board for that site can ride with the notice. Email the PDF, the address, and the due date. The drive is I-5 north.

Common questions

Is Smokey Point a Marysville sign or an Arlington sign?

It depends on the parcel. Smokey Point straddles Marysville and Arlington. The letter names which city. Do not put a Marysville header on an Arlington lot.

Is the Tulalip Reservation a City of Marysville notice?

No. The reservation is west of I-5 and is not City of Marysville. Say so in the email if that is the site. State Avenue and downtown are the city.

What do you need before printing a Marysville leasing sign?

The address, the file, and whether it is a yard panel, a window, or both. Add the due date. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.
