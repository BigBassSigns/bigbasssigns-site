Yarrow Point is the easternmost of the three points on this part of Lake Washington, and nearly all of the town sits north of SR-520. Hunts Point is the next point west. Clyde Hill is south of the freeway. Kirkland borders the town on the east, along Yarrow Bay. From the Lynnwood shop it is about thirteen miles, I-5 south and 520 east.

The header is Town of Yarrow Point. Bellevue and Kirkland are the wrong city even when the street feels like the same neighborhood. Medina, Hunts Point, and Clyde Hill each have their own header too. Outside the town limits, the board is King County.

These are usually one residential notice. Email the PDF, the address, and the due date. We print in Lynnwood and post Yarrow Point on the 520 loop. The spec is the land use sign packet. A county letter uses the King County notice board.

Common questions

Can you print a Bellevue or Kirkland header for Yarrow Point?

No. Yarrow Point is its own town. The header follows the letter. Medina, Hunts Point, and Clyde Hill are not substitutes. Outside the town, the board is King County.

What should I email for a Yarrow Point notice?

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the notice PDF, the address in the town, the due date, and whether the job is install or board-only.

How do you reach Yarrow Point from Lynnwood?

South on I-5 and east on SR-520. It is a scheduled post, often with Hunts Point and Clyde Hill. Permanent boards take two to three days.
