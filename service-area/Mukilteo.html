Mukilteo

Land use signs and construction signs in Mukilteo.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Mukilteo is the waterfront city west of Lynnwood, on Possession Sound. The ferry to Clinton leaves from the bottom of the Mukilteo Speedway (SR 525). Harbour Pointe sits on the hill above Old Town. The shop is about six miles east of that waterfront, in Lynnwood.

A site inside the city takes a City of Mukilteo header. Edmonds to the south and Everett to the north are different cities, and a board does not borrow their letterhead. If the parcel is outside Mukilteo, the header is Snohomish County.

Send the notice PDF, the street address, and the date the board has to be up. We print in Lynnwood and post along the Speedway. Old Town and the ferry dock are a tighter stop than the plateau. Say whether you need install or board-only. A banner for a Speedway storefront prints in the same shop.

Common questions

Who is the jurisdiction on a Mukilteo land use sign?

City of Mukilteo when the site is inside the city. Snohomish County when it is not. Edmonds and Everett headers do not go on a Mukilteo parcel.

What should I send before you print?

The notice PDF, the site address, and the date it has to be up. Add install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Can you post near the ferry the same day?

A clean temp board can go out the same day. Mukilteo is about six miles from the Lynnwood shop. Ferry traffic at the bottom of the Speedway can change the window. Permanent boards take two to three days.
