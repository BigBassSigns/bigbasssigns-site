Renton lines the south end of Lake Washington along I-405, about 25 miles from Lynnwood in a straight line. Downtown and The Landing are City of Renton commercial streets. A leasing panel or a construction board there uses the City of Renton header when the letter says Renton.

Printing stays at the Lynnwood shop. The header on the board is City of Renton when the letter says Renton. Parcels outside the city, including unincorporated land on the plateau east of Renton, follow King County. Tukwila, to the west, is a separate city.

Send the address and the file for a storefront or leasing panel, a construction sign, or a land use sign. When the board is a King County notice and we install it, the affidavit photo goes back within 7 days.

Common questions

Where are Renton signs printed?

In the Lynnwood shop. City of Renton is the header on the board when that is the letter. King County is the header on unincorporated parcels.

What should a Renton leasing or construction order include?

The address, the file or notice PDF, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How fast can you post in Renton?

Temp boards the same day when the file is clean and in early. The drive is I-405 south. Permanent boards are two to three days.
