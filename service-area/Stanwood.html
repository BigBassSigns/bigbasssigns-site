Stanwood

Construction signs and land use signs in Stanwood.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote (425) 405-0118

Stanwood is the north Snohomish County city on the Stillaguamish, west of I-5, where the road continues onto Camano Island. A City of Stanwood permit uses a City of Stanwood header. Unincorporated land around the city is Snohomish County. An address on Camano Island is Island County, not Stanwood.

The shop is in Lynnwood, south on I-5. Email the address, the county named on the letter, and the date the board has to be up. A construction site sign and a land use sign for the same job can be printed in one order.

Common questions

Is a Camano Island board a Stanwood sign?

No. Camano Island is in Island County. Stanwood is a Snohomish County city. The header follows the letter.

What should I send for a Stanwood quote?

The site address, the notice PDF, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Do City of Stanwood and Snohomish County share one board?

No. They are printed as separate boards. Check the jurisdiction on the letter before the file is sent.
