SeaTac

Storefront signs and directional signs in SeaTac.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

SeaTac is the city along International Boulevard, SR 99, about 27 miles south of the Lynnwood shop in a straight line. Hotel and restaurant fronts on that boulevard take storefront lettering and directional signs. When the parcel is inside the city, the header is City of SeaTac.

Tukwila is to the east. Burien is to the west. Each city keeps the header on its own letter. An unincorporated King County parcel uses the county notice. All of it prints in the Lynnwood shop.

Email the address and the art for storefront lettering, a directional sign, a banner, or a land use sign.

Common questions

Is a SeaTac sign a Tukwila sign?

No. City of SeaTac, City of Tukwila, and City of Burien are different headers. International Boulevard inside SeaTac uses City of SeaTac.

What should a SeaTac storefront order include?

The address on International Boulevard or the side street, the file, and whether you need window lettering, a directional sign, a banner, or a land use board. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How fast can you post in SeaTac?

Temp boards the same day when the file is in early. The drive is I-5 south. Afternoon traffic adds time. Permanent boards are two to three days.
