Clyde Hill

Construction signs and land use signs in Clyde Hill.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Clyde Hill is the hilltop city east of Medina. 84th Avenue NE is the west city line, shared with Medina, and Bellevue is on the east line. SR-520 runs along the north end of town. Yarrow Point and Hunts Point are on the north side of that freeway. It is about fourteen miles from the Lynnwood shop. Two sites a few blocks apart can be Clyde Hill, Medina, or Bellevue, and the headers are not interchangeable.

City of Clyde Hill goes on a site inside the city. King County goes on a site outside it. Send the letter with the file so the header is set before we print. Land use notices follow the land use sign spec. A job-site board follows the construction sign spec.

We print in Lynnwood and post Clyde Hill with Medina and Yarrow Point on the same SR-520 trip. Put the due date in the email. Permanent boards take two to three days. A county letter uses the King County notice board.

Common questions

Which header goes on a Clyde Hill site?

City of Clyde Hill when the site is inside the city. King County when it is not. Medina, Yarrow Point, Hunts Point, and Bellevue are different jurisdictions.

What should I send with the artwork?

The site address, the letter or notice PDF, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com. The letter is what keeps a Clyde Hill board from being printed as Bellevue.

Can a Clyde Hill post share the SR-520 loop?

Yes. Clyde Hill is posted with Medina and Yarrow Point on the SR-520 trip from Lynnwood. Put the due date in the email so it can share that loop.
