Issaquah

Land use notices and construction signs in Issaquah.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Issaquah sits at the south end of Lake Sammamish on I-90, about 23 miles from the Lynnwood shop in a straight line. The drive is I-405 to I-90. Front Street is the old downtown. The Highlands are the plateau neighborhoods above town. Both take a City of Issaquah header when the parcel is inside the city.

Sammamish, north along the lake, is a separate city with its own letter. Parcels outside Issaquah city limits, including land east toward Preston, follow the King County notice. Send the letter with the PDF so the board matches the jurisdiction.

A land use sign, a construction site sign, and a banner for the same Issaquah site can go on one order. Email the packet to Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Common questions

Is an Issaquah notice a King County board?

Only when the letter says King County. Front Street and the Highlands use City of Issaquah when the parcel is inside the city. Sammamish has its own header.

What should the Issaquah packet include?

Site address, notice PDF, due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How fast can you post in Issaquah?

Temp boards the same day when the file is clean and in early. The drive is I-405 to I-90. Permanent boards are two to three days. When the board is a King County notice and install is part of the job, the affidavit photo goes back within 7 days of posting.
