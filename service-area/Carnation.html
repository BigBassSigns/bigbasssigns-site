Carnation

Main Street signs and public notices in Carnation.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Carnation is the small city on the Tolt River, south of Duvall on SR 203 and north of Fall City. Tolt Avenue is the main street, a few blocks rather than a corridor. The shop is about 23 miles west in Lynnwood. The drive runs through Woodinville and the valley, and it is a longer day than Duvall.

Commercial work on Tolt Avenue is window lettering, a leasing panel for an empty space, and an A-frame when a stand on that street needs one. Order them together if they share a due date. A house or a lot off the avenue is a property sign or a construction notice, not a retail file.

City of Carnation is the header only inside the city. Fall City is unincorporated, and the fields between Carnation and Duvall are often King County. Those boards use the county notice. Duvall's city letter does not go on a Carnation parcel. When install is part of a county notice, we photograph the board after it is posted. King County wants that photo back within 7 days. Email the file, the address, and the date.

Common questions

Is Carnation the same notice as Duvall or Fall City?

No. Carnation is its own city. Duvall has a city letter. Fall City is unincorporated King County. The packet names which board to print.

What commercial signs fit Tolt Avenue?

Window lettering, a leasing panel for an open space, and an A-frame. The avenue is short. Send the address so we are not guessing which block.

Can a Carnation board still post on a short timeline?

A clean temp board can go the same day, with the valley drive built into the day. Carnation is about 23 miles from Lynnwood. Permanent boards take two to three days.
