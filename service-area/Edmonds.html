Edmonds

Construction signs and land use signs for Edmonds sites.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Edmonds is the city west of Lynnwood, out Highway 99 and down to the waterfront and the SR-104 ferry. A City of Edmonds permit uses a City of Edmonds header. Unincorporated land past the city limits is Snohomish County. The letter names which header to print.

Email the site address, the jurisdiction on the letter, and the date the board has to be up. We print in Lynnwood and post on the west run. Pair the land use sign with a construction site sign when the site is already fenced.

Common questions

Do City of Edmonds and Snohomish County share a land use sign?

No. The header follows the letter. City of Edmonds and Snohomish County are printed as separate boards.

What do you need for an Edmonds quote?

The address, the due date, the notice PDF or artwork, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Can a temporary board go up the same day in Edmonds?

Yes when the file is clean and the truck is free. Edmonds is a short run west of the Lynnwood shop. Permanent boards are usually two to three days.
