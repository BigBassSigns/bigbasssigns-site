Lake Forest Park

Land use signs and construction signs in Lake Forest Park.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Lake Forest Park is north King County, on SR-522 between Shoreline and Kenmore. It is a short south run from the Lynnwood shop. The notice is City of Lake Forest Park unless the parcel is unincorporated King County. Those boards are not interchangeable.

When the job moves from the hearing to the build, the land use sign and a construction site sign can post on one trip. Email the packet and the date it has to be up.

Common questions

Is Lake Forest Park a King County board or a city board?

City of Lake Forest Park when the site is inside the city. King County when the parcel is unincorporated. Check the letter before we print the header.

What do I send for a Lake Forest Park notice?

Site address, notice PDF, due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Can the land use sign and the construction sign go up together?

Yes. Say so in the email and we schedule both on the same run from Lynnwood.
