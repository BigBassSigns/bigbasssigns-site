Beaux Arts Village

Property signs and town notices in Beaux Arts Village.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Beaux Arts Village is a town of about 310 people on the east shore of Lake Washington, just north of the I-90 East Channel bridge. Bellevue surrounds it on the north, south, and east. The shore is the west line. There is no commercial street. Downtown Bellevue is a few miles north. The shop is in Lynnwood, about 17 miles away.

Do not send a retail leasing sign or a storefront for an address inside the town. There is nowhere to hang it. If the building is actually in Bellevue, say so. That order is Bellevue window lettering and a City of Bellevue header. Inside the town, the job is a property sign on a house or a notice for work on a lot.

The letter has to say Town of Beaux Arts Village. A City of Bellevue header is the wrong board, even though Bellevue is on three sides. King County is the wrong board too. Email the notice PDF and the address. We print in Lynnwood and come in from I-90 at the East Channel. A construction sign for a remodel goes on the same visit when you need one.

Common questions

Can you print a storefront sign for Beaux Arts Village?

The town has no commercial street. A storefront or retail leasing sign belongs to a Bellevue address, and that header is City of Bellevue. Inside the town, we print property signs and town notices.

Is Beaux Arts Village part of Bellevue for the notice header?

No. The town is its own jurisdiction, surrounded by Bellevue. The board says Town of Beaux Arts Village when the parcel is in the town.

How do you reach Beaux Arts Village from Lynnwood?

I-405 to I-90, then the East Channel. It is about 17 miles. A clean temp board can go the same day. Permanent boards take two to three days.
