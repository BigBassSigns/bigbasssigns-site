Duvall

Storefront signs and land use notices in Duvall.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Duvall is the valley town on SR 203, east of Woodinville and north of Carnation. Main Street is the commercial block. Newer plats sit on the hills above that street. From the Lynnwood shop it is about 17 miles: SR 522 to the valley, then SR 203.

A Main Street tenant usually needs window lettering, sometimes a banner on the front, and a leasing panel when the space is empty. Weekend retail and farm stands on that same street often add an A-frame. Say which of those is in the order. A house on the hill is a yard sign, not a storefront file.

Print the header the letter uses. City of Duvall covers Main Street and the plats inside the city limits. Much of the valley floor around the city is still unincorporated King County, and that board is the county notice. Carnation and Woodinville do not share Duvall's letterhead. When the board is a King County notice and install is part of the job, we photograph it after it is in the ground. King County wants that affidavit photo back within 7 days.

Common questions

Is a Duvall notice the same as a King County notice?

Only when the letter says King County. Main Street and the plats inside the city use City of Duvall. Lots outside the city limits use the county board.

What should a Duvall storefront order include?

The address on Main Street or the side street, the file, and whether you need window lettering, a banner, a leasing sign, or an A-frame. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How do you reach Duvall from Lynnwood?

SR 522 to the valley, then SR 203. It is about 17 miles. A clean temp board can still go the same day. Permanent boards take two to three days. Friday afternoon traffic in the valley is the usual delay.
