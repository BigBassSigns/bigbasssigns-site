Gold Bar is the small city on US 2 in eastern Snohomish County, along the Skykomish River, east of Sultan and west of Index. A site inside the city uses the City of Gold Bar header. Land outside the city limits is Snohomish County. The letter decides which header is printed.

The shop is in Lynnwood. Gold Bar is the east run on US 2, past Monroe. Email the address, the PDF, and the due date. A land use sign and a construction site sign can go out in the same order.

Common questions

Is a Gold Bar sign a Snohomish County board?

Only when the letter is Snohomish County. Inside the city, the header is City of Gold Bar. Sultan and Index are different places.

What do you need to print a Gold Bar board?

The site address, the notice PDF, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Where is Gold Bar relative to the shop?

Gold Bar is east of Monroe on US 2, along the Skykomish River. The board is printed in Lynnwood. Email the due date with the file.
