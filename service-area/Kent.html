Kent

Construction signs and public notice signs in Kent.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote (425) 405-0118

Kent is the King County city in the Green River valley, along I-5 and SR-167, between Renton and Auburn. Downtown, the valley floor, East Hill, and West Hill use the City of Kent header when that is the letter. Renton, Auburn, Tukwila, and unincorporated King County are different boards.

The shop is in Lynnwood. Email the address, whether it is in the valley or on the hill, the notice PDF, and the due date. A project board and a land use sign can be printed in the same order. Say install or board-only.

Common questions

Does East Hill use a different sign header than downtown Kent?

No. East Hill, West Hill, and the valley are City of Kent when the letter says so. A King County letter is a county board, even when the site is near the city.

What do I email to start a Kent sign?

The site address, the notice PDF, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

When is the board a King County notice instead of a City of Kent sign?

When the letter is King County. City of Kent and King County are printed as separate boards in Lynnwood.
