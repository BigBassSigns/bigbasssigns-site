Sultan

Public notice signs and construction boards in Sultan.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Sultan is the US 2 city east of Monroe, in the Skykomish River valley, about 23 miles from Lynnwood in a straight line. The drive runs through Monroe. Weekend traffic toward Stevens Pass is the usual delay, so send a tight due date before Friday afternoon.

City of Sultan covers the blocks in town. Startup and the other valley parcels outside the city limits are Snohomish County, and those boards follow the county public notice. A Monroe letter stays with a Monroe address.

Email the letter with the PDF. Say whether the job is a land use sign, a construction sign, or the Snohomish County public notice board.

Common questions

Is a Sultan notice a Monroe notice?

No. Monroe is the city to the west on US 2. A Sultan address uses City of Sultan. Valley parcels outside the city limits use Snohomish County.

What should the Sultan packet include?

Site address, notice PDF, due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How fast can you post in Sultan?

Temp boards the same day when the file is in early and US 2 is moving. Permanent boards are two to three days. Friday traffic toward Stevens Pass is the usual delay.
