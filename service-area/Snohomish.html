City of Snohomish

City of Snohomish land use signs, not the county board.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

The City of Snohomish and Snohomish County share a name. They do not share a sign header. The city is the river town on US 2, about twelve miles east of the Lynnwood shop, between Everett and Monroe. First Street and the downtown grid are inside the city. Land past the city line is the county.

Write the jurisdiction in the email. The word Snohomish alone is not enough to print. Send the letter, the notice PDF, the site address, and the date it has to be up, and say install or board-only. We print in Lynnwood and post this city on the US 2 run with Everett and Monroe.

A downtown banner prints in the same shop. The county product, when that is the letter, is the Snohomish County public notice. The city product is the land use sign.

Common questions

Is the City of Snohomish the same board as Snohomish County?

No. The names match and the headers do not. The letter names the jurisdiction. City sites get City of Snohomish. Land outside the city gets the Snohomish County public notice.

What do I need to send so the header is right?

The letter that names the jurisdiction, the notice PDF, the site address, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Is the city of Snohomish on the Monroe route?

Yes. It sits on US 2 between Everett and Monroe, about twelve miles east of the Lynnwood shop. We print here and post it on that run. It is not a few-minute stop the way Mountlake Terrace is.
