Bellevue

Property signs and construction notices in Bellevue.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Bellevue is the Eastside city south of Kirkland and west of Redmond, about 17 miles from Lynnwood. Downtown, Bel-Red, Wilburton, and Factoria are different stops on one I-405 loop. The printers stay in Lynnwood. We do not run a Bellevue shop. The drive is I-405, or SR 522 over to I-405 when that road is moving.

A Bellevue leasing sign is usually a retail bay or an office floor, not a lawn stake. Send the building, the suite, and the date the space has to be marked. Window lettering for the tenant at street level can be the same order as the panel at the door. A house listing is a yard sign and a different file.

Construction notices and public notice boards say City of Bellevue when that is the letter. King County is a different board, used only when the packet says King County. Kirkland, Redmond, Newcastle, and Beaux Arts Village each keep their own header. An Eastside job does not borrow a neighbor's letterhead. Email the packet with install or board-only. A project board and the notice can post on one trip.

Common questions

Is a Bellevue construction notice a King County board?

Not when the letter is City of Bellevue. Downtown, Bel-Red, Wilburton, and Factoria follow the city letter. Use the King County notice only when the packet says King County.

Can a leasing panel and window lettering be one Bellevue order?

Yes. Send the suite, the file for the panel, and the file for the glass. Both print in Lynnwood. Say if the glass is a separate visit from the panel.

How fast can you post in Bellevue?

A clean temp board can go out the same day. Bellevue is about 17 miles south of the shop, and I-405 sets the window. Permanent boards take two to three days.
