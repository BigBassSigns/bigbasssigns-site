Langley is the small city on the south end of Whidbey Island, on Saratoga Passage. About 15 miles is the straight-line distance from Lynnwood. The trip from the shop is the Mukilteo-Clinton ferry, then south on the island. A City of Langley letter is not an Island County letter. Coupeville and Oak Harbor are farther north on the island.

Email the site address, the notice PDF or the storefront artwork, and the date it has to be up. The shop prints in Lynnwood. A land use sign, a construction site sign, a banner, or an A-frame for a downtown storefront can be ordered from the same shop. Say whether the job is install or pickup.

Common questions

Is the trip from Lynnwood 15 miles?

About 15 miles is the straight-line distance. The trip from the Lynnwood shop uses the Mukilteo-Clinton ferry and then the road south to Langley. Email the due date so that run can be planned.

Is a Langley sign an Island County board?

Only when the letter is Island County. Inside the city, the header is City of Langley. The Town of Coupeville and the City of Oak Harbor are separate from Langley.

What do I email for a Langley sign?

The site address, the PDF or artwork, the due date, and install, pickup, or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.
