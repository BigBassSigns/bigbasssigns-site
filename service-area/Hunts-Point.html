Hunts Point

Public notice signs in Hunts Point.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Hunts Point is a residential town of a few hundred people on the middle point of Lake Washington, west of Yarrow Point and east of Medina's Evergreen Point. The town sits just north of SR-520. The shop is in Lynnwood, about thirteen miles away. The work here is a single notice board on a house street, not a storefront run.

Town of Hunts Point is the header. A Kirkland header is the wrong city. Kirkland is farther east, past Yarrow Point, and Yarrow Point itself is a different town. If the letter is King County, print the county notice instead.

Email the PDF, the address, and the due date, and say install or board-only. We print in Lynnwood. Hunts Point is a scheduled stop on the SR-520 loop, not a same-hour errand from the shop. Start from the land use sign spec unless the letter is the King County notice board.

Common questions

Is Hunts Point part of Kirkland for sign headers?

No. Hunts Point is its own town. Kirkland, Yarrow Point, and King County each take the header on the letter.

What do I send for a Hunts Point board?

Email the notice PDF, the house address in Hunts Point, and the date the board has to be standing. Say install or board-only. Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Are Hunts Point signs usually one board?

Yes. The town is a few hundred people, and the sign is a residential notice. We print it in Lynnwood and post it on the SR-520 loop. Same-day only when the file is already clean and the loop is running.
