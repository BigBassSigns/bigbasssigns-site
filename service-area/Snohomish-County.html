Common questions

How do I know if the board is city or Snohomish County?

The letter in the packet names the jurisdiction. City of Lynnwood, City of Everett, and Snohomish County are not the same header. Email the letter with the PDF.

Where are Snohomish County notice boards printed?

In the Lynnwood shop. We post from there. County parcels use the Snohomish County public notice sign.

Which Snohomish cities have their own page?

Lynnwood, Everett, Mill Creek, Monroe, Mountlake Terrace, Edmonds, Brier, and Woodway. Bothell is on the county line and has its own page. The other incorporated cities in the county are printed from this same Lynnwood shop.
