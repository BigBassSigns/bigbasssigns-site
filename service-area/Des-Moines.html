Des Moines

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote (425) 405-0118

Des Moines is the King County city on Puget Sound between Normandy Park and Federal Way. Pacific Highway S runs through town, and the marina is on the water. A City of Des Moines letter is not a SeaTac letter, not a Kent letter, and not a King County letter. The header on the notice is the one that gets printed.

The shop is in Lynnwood. Email the site address, the PDF, and the due date. A land use sign and a construction site sign for the same parcel can be printed together. Name the city if the artwork is a banner for a Pacific Highway storefront.

Common questions

Is a Des Moines notice a SeaTac sign?

No. Des Moines and SeaTac are different cities. Kent, Normandy Park, Federal Way, and unincorporated King County each follow their own letter.

What goes in the Des Moines packet?

Site address, notice PDF, due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Which board is used if the parcel is outside Des Moines?

The letter names it. When the letter is King County, the board is the King County notice, printed in Lynnwood.
