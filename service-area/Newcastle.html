Newcastle

Property signs and public notice boards in Newcastle.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Newcastle is the ridge city south of Bellevue, about 22 miles from Lynnwood. Coal Creek Parkway is the main road and the only real retail stretch. There is no separate downtown. Renton is southwest and Issaquah is east. The drive from the shop is I-405 south, then the parkway up the hill.

A leasing panel or window lettering is for a suite on Coal Creek Parkway. A house on the ridge is a property sign, and it should not arrive as a storefront file. Put the address in the email so those two jobs stay apart.

Inside the city, the notice says City of Newcastle. Bellevue to the north and Renton to the south are different cities. Land outside the limits, including parts of May Valley, is a King County notice. If that is the packet, we print the county board and photograph it after install. King County wants the affidavit photo back within 7 days. A construction sign for the same site can go up on that visit.

Common questions

Is Newcastle a Bellevue sign?

No. Newcastle is its own city south of Bellevue. City of Newcastle, City of Bellevue, and King County are different headers. The letter picks the board.

Where are the storefronts in Newcastle?

On Coal Creek Parkway. The rest of the city is houses. A leasing panel for a parkway suite and a property sign for a ridge lot are different orders. Send the address.

How fast can a Newcastle notice be posted?

A clean temp board can go the same day. Newcastle is about 22 miles from the Lynnwood shop, south on I-405 and up Coal Creek Parkway. Permanent boards take two to three days.
