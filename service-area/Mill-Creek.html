Mill Creek

Mill Creek is a regular drive, not a keyword we invented.

Print and install from the Lynnwood shop. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com with the address and due date.

Mill Creek sits inside the everyday radius from the Lynnwood shop — school drop-offs and errands included. Land-use and construction boards here are usually Snohomish County or City of Mill Creek forms. Send whichever PDF the planner attached.

Same-day temp boards when the file is ready. Two to three days for a finished 4×8. If the site is inside the city vs. unincorporated, say that in the first email so the header on the board is the right one.
