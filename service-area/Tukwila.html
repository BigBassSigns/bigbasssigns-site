Tukwila is the city at the I-5, I-405, and SR 518 junction, about 24 miles south of the Lynnwood shop in a straight line. Southcenter is the retail district. A storefront or a leasing panel there takes a City of Tukwila header when the parcel is inside the city. Seattle, north of the city line, uses the City of Seattle header when that is the letter.

The work prints in Lynnwood. A storefront file, a banner, and a construction sign are separate lines on the same order. An unincorporated parcel uses the King County notice board.

Renton is the city to the east. SeaTac and Burien sit on the other side of the freeway junction. Each address keeps the header on its own letter.

Common questions

Is a Tukwila sign a Seattle sign?

No. City of Tukwila, City of Seattle, City of Renton, and King County each take the header on the letter. Southcenter uses City of Tukwila when the parcel is inside Tukwila.

What should a Tukwila storefront or job site order include?

The address, the file or notice PDF, the due date, and whether you need storefront lettering, a leasing panel, a banner, or a construction board. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How fast can you post in Tukwila?

Temp boards the same day when the file is clean and in early. The drive is I-5 south, and afternoon traffic adds time. Permanent boards are two to three days.
