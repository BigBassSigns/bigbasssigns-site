Everett

Everett job sites. Snohomish County packets. Lynnwood print.

Print and install from the Lynnwood shop. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com with the address and due date.

Everett sits inside the standard install radius. Snohomish County land-use boards for sites in and around the city follow the PDS notice package — often 4×8 on 4×4 posts, midpoint of frontage, affidavit photo after it is in the ground.

City of Everett projects still need the actual notice text. Send that PDF. Do not ask us to invent the wording.

Drive time from the shop is short enough for a same-day temp board when the file is ready. If you are already on the north end of town, say so — the install window is the afternoon, not rush-hour I-5 theater.
