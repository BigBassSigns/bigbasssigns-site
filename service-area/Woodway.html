Woodway

Construction signs and land use signs in Woodway.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Woodway is a small residential city between Edmonds and Shoreline, a few minutes from the Lynnwood shop. A Woodway parcel gets a City of Woodway header. An Edmonds header on that board is the wrong jurisdiction. Land outside the city is Snohomish County.

These are usually one board. Email the address, the jurisdiction on the letter, and the due date. The spec is the same land use sign packet we print for the rest of south Snohomish County.

Common questions

Can you print an Edmonds header for a Woodway site?

No. Woodway and Edmonds are different cities. The header follows the city on the letter. Outside the city limits, the board is Snohomish County.

What do I email for a Woodway sign?

The site address, the notice PDF, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Do you install in Woodway?

Yes. Install, pickup, or board-only. Woodway is a short run from the Lynnwood shop, usually with Edmonds or Shoreline on the same loop.
