Mercer Island is the city in Lake Washington on I-90, about 19 miles from Lynnwood. The island is the city. There is no unincorporated lot to mis-header, and there is no Bellevue or Seattle land on the island. Town Center, around 77th and 78th Avenues SE and SE 27th Street, is the commercial block. The rest of the island is houses.

Leasing signs and window lettering belong at Town Center. Send the suite. A house north or south of that district is a property sign or a construction notice, not a retail window. Say which stop it is so the trip is not two loops across the bridge.

The notice header is City of Mercer Island. A Bellevue letter from across the East Channel, or a Seattle letter from across the floating bridge, does not go on an island parcel. We print in Lynnwood and reach the island on I-405 to I-90. Email the file, the address, and install or board-only. A construction sign for the same site can post with the notice.

Common questions

Is Mercer Island a Bellevue or Seattle notice?

Neither. The island is City of Mercer Island. Bellevue is across the East Channel. Seattle is across I-90. The board uses the island's letter.

Where do storefront and leasing signs go on Mercer Island?

Town Center, around 77th and 78th Avenues SE and SE 27th Street. Houses elsewhere on the island are property signs or construction notices, not retail windows.

What should a Mercer Island packet include?

The notice PDF or the art file, the street address, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.
