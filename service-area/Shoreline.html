Common questions

Is a Shoreline notice a Seattle sign?

No. Shoreline is its own city. Seattle, Shoreline, and unincorporated King County each take the header on the letter.

What should the Shoreline packet include?

Site address, notice PDF, due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How fast can you post in Shoreline?

Temp boards as soon as the same day when the file is clean. Shoreline is a short run south of Lynnwood. Permanent boards are two to three days.
