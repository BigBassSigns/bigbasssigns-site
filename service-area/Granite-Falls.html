Granite Falls

Land use signs and job site boards in Granite Falls.

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Request a quote

Granite Falls is the SR 92 city northeast of Lynnwood, about 24 miles in a straight line, where the road turns toward the Mountain Loop. The shop prints in Lynnwood and posts from there. The drive is north, then east on SR 92.

Inside the city limits the header is City of Granite Falls. Outside those limits the parcel is usually Snohomish County. Arlington, to the northwest, is a separate city with its own letter. Send the letter with the file so the board matches the address.

Order a land use sign or a construction sign for a city job. County parcels use the Snohomish County public notice board.

Common questions

Is Granite Falls a Snohomish County notice?

Only outside the city limits. The city itself uses City of Granite Falls. County parcels use the Snohomish County public notice.

What should the Granite Falls packet include?

Site address, notice PDF, due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How do you reach Granite Falls from Lynnwood?

North, then east on SR 92. It is about 24 miles in a straight line. Temp boards go the same day when the file is clean and in early. Permanent boards are two to three days.
