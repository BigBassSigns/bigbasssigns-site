Normandy Park is the King County city on Puget Sound, south of Burien and north of Des Moines. A site inside the city uses the City of Normandy Park header. Burien, Des Moines, and unincorporated King County are different letters. Read the header on the notice before the file is printed.

The shop is in Lynnwood. Email the site address, the notice PDF, and the date the board has to be up. A land use sign and a construction site sign for the same parcel can be printed in one order.

Common questions

Is a Normandy Park notice a Des Moines or Burien sign?

No. Normandy Park is its own city. Des Moines, Burien, and unincorporated King County each take the header on the letter.

What should the Normandy Park packet include?

Site address, notice PDF, due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Do you print a King County board for a Normandy Park address?

Only when the letter is King County. A City of Normandy Park letter is a city board. Both are printed in Lynnwood.
