Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the address and due date.

Kenmore sits on the north shore of Lake Washington, where the Sammamish River meets the lake, about six miles south of the Lynnwood shop. SR-522, Bothell Way, runs through town between Lake Forest Park and Bothell. Saint Edward State Park and Log Boom Park are on this shoreline. Kenmore Air flies off the lake, so the address has to be right before anyone drives down.

City of Kenmore is the header when the letter says Kenmore. The city incorporated out of unincorporated King County, and the county board is still the right one when the parcel is outside the city limits. Do not print a Lake Forest Park or Bothell header on a Kenmore site.

Email the PDF and the due date. We print in Lynnwood and run Kenmore with Lake Forest Park and Bothell. A city letter follows the land use sign spec. A county letter is the King County notice board. A banner for a Bothell Way storefront is the same shop.

Common questions

Is a Kenmore sign a King County board?

Only when the letter is King County. A site inside the city is City of Kenmore. Lake Forest Park and Bothell are separate headers.

What do I email to start a Kenmore board?

The site address, the notice PDF, the due date, and install or board-only. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

Do you photograph King County posts in Kenmore?

Yes, when install is part of the job. We photograph the board after it is in the ground. King County wants that affidavit photo back within 7 days of posting.
