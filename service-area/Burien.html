Burien is southwest of Seattle, about 25 miles from Lynnwood in a straight line. Downtown runs along SW 152nd Street, between 1st Avenue South and Ambaum Boulevard. Those storefronts use a City of Burien header. The shop that prints them is in Lynnwood.

White Center, just north of the city line, is unincorporated King County. A board there is the county notice. SeaTac, to the south, is its own city and keeps its own letter. Send the letter that names the jurisdiction.

Email window lettering, an A-frame size, a banner, or a land use sign PDF. Say install or pickup.

Common questions

Is downtown Burien a King County notice?

No. SW 152nd Street inside the city is City of Burien. White Center, north of the city line, is unincorporated King County and uses the county board.

What should a Burien order include?

The address, the file or notice PDF, the due date, and whether you need window lettering, a banner, an A-frame, or a land use board. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com.

How fast can you post in Burien?

Temp boards the same day when the file is in early. The drive from Lynnwood is I-5 south, then west. Permanent boards are two to three days.
