Don’t let a sign delay your entitlement.

The comment period does not start until the land use sign is posted correctly. Wrong font, wrong setback, or a late install slips the hearing.

We print land use signs and public notice signs to the current city spec — Seattle SDCI and Master Use Permit (MUP) boards, Snohomish County land use action signs, and city boards in Lynnwood, Everett, Redmond, Sammamish, Woodinville, and nearby.

The legal details

A Seattle MUP sign needs a specific digital map. A Snohomish County land use notice may need to be mailed to you. We pull the current code for the address. Header language, letter height, and map scale change by jurisdiction.
