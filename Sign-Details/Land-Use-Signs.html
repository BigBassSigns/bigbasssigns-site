Land use signs

Land use signs posted on time — without you learning every city’s spec.

A land use sign has to match the Notice of Application: size, header, posts, and setback. That changes by city and county. Forward the packet. We print the public notice board and install it.

Email the notice package

Built for planners, developers, and engineers

The completeness letter gives you a short clock. The land use sign is how the comment period starts. You should not keep a binder of King County Permitting and Snohomish County PDS posting rules. That is the shop’s job.

You get a weatherproof public notice board, a plumb install, and a photo for the affidavit of posting. If wind or a hit-and-run takes it down, we put it back. When the planner releases it, we pull the posts.

King County, Snohomish County, Island, Skagit, Whatcom, and the cities inside them.

What to send

  • Site address and jurisdiction
  • File or permit number
  • The notice PDF
  • The date it has to be up
  • Install, or board-only
