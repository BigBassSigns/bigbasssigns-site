Forward the packet. We post the board.

A land use sign starts the comment period. Size, header, posts, and setback change by city and county. You should not keep a binder of King County Permitting and Snohomish County PDS posting rules. That is the shop’s job.

Weatherproof public notice board, a plumb install, and a photo for the affidavit of posting. If wind or a hit-and-run takes it down, we put it back. When the file is released, we pull the posts.

King County, Snohomish County, Island, Skagit, Whatcom, and the cities inside them — including Lynnwood, Everett, Redmond, Sammamish, Woodinville, Mill Creek, Bothell, Kirkland, and Monroe.

Common questions

What should I email for a land use or public notice board?

Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the site address and jurisdiction, file or permit number, the notice PDF, the date it has to be up, and whether you need install or board-only. For land-use and public notice, attach the PDF — that is the whole packet.

Do you install, or can I get the board only?

Say install, or board-only. Pickup, ship, or on-site install are all options. We print the notice from the packet and can put the board in the ground when you need install.

Which counties do you cover?

Lynnwood, Snohomish County, and King County packets are the usual jobs. We also cover Island, Skagit, Whatcom, and the cities inside them.

Can you do same-day boards?

Temp boards as soon as same day when the file is clean. Permanent boards in two to three days.
