What this page is

High-visibility construction safety signs for the site perimeter and the trailer door. Not a substitute for your safety officer’s poster list — a way to get the headers printed on stock that does not curl in Snohomish rain.

If you also need a weatherproof board for wage-rate and employment-law posters, say so in the email. That is a kiosk / covered board, not a 10-inch vinyl sticker.
