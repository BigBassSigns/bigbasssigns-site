Project boards

4×4 and 4×8 construction signs in the ground.

This is the money work: a board at the gate with the project name, the contacts, and the logos the GC already approved. We print it, we set it, we pull it when the job is done.

Print, install, remove

Supers do not want a crate of coroplast and a shopping list. They want the construction sign standing before the inspector walks. Tell us the size — most jobs are 4×4 or 4×8 — the post spec if the owner has one, and whether we come back for removal.

MDO and corrugated plastic for boards that stay. Coroplast when the clock is short. Vinyl faces and lamination when the logo has to survive a winter.

What to send

  • Site address
  • 4×4, 4×8, or custom
  • Install yes or no
  • Remove later yes or no
  • Artwork or a text list
  • Due date
