Project boards

Construction site signs without pulling a carpenter off the wall.

Project identification, company boards, and perimeter signs on 4×4 and 4×8 stock. We print in Lynnwood, dig the holes, and come back when the job no longer needs the board.

Typical boards

GC / owner project ID. Subcontractor lists. Hours and gate rules. “Authorized personnel.” Construction entrance. Those 4×8 faces that have to survive one more winter on 522.

Materials: MDO, exterior plywood, coroplast for short-run temp work, mesh when it hangs on a fence instead of posts. Vinyl faces and lamination for rain.

Temp boards can move the same day. A painted-looking permanent board is usually a two- to three-day print.

Install

  • Two 4×4 posts, set to stand in wind
  • Fence-mount when posts are the wrong call
  • Photo back if the GC or city wants proof
  • Take-down and haul-off

Send size, due date, and whether we set posts.
