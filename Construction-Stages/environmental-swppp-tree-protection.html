SWPPP signs

SWPPP signs the inspector can read at the gate.

A SWPPP sign — also searched as SW3P — is the stormwater contact board your plan calls for: project name, permit number, after-hours phone. Not a land use sign. Not an OSHA header.

Email the contact block

SWPPP contact signs

Ecology and city inspectors look for the SWPPP sign at the entrance. A paper printout in a sheet protector is how that board disappears in the first storm. We print a durable face and set it where the plan shows.

Tree protection signs

Tree protection signs and limits-of-disturbance faces mark save-trees so a sub does not stage lumber in the root zone. Same visit as the SWPPP sign when the dates line up.
