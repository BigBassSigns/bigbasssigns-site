About

A Lynnwood print shop that also sets the posts.

Big Bass Signs prints land use signs, construction boards, banners, and custom lettering — then installs them across Western Washington so your crew stays on their own work.

The shop is in Lynnwood. Jobs run the I-5 / 405 / 522 corridor and farther on multi-site work. Chris Strandin owns it. The printers are two Roland VG3s and a Seal 54E laminator. The truck carries finished boards and post-hole tools.

Most of the work is regulation-dependent signage: public notice and land use boards, SWPPP contact signs, project ID, and the banners and lettering that sit next to them. You forward the packet or the artwork. We match it and hang it.

Local to Lynnwood, Everett, Redmond, Sammamish, Woodinville, and Mill Creek — and happy to drive for a board that has to be up this week.
