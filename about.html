A local sign business you can call — and someone who lives here answers.

The process should stay easy: send the file or the packet, get a board, a banner, or lettering back that is ready to go up. You are not handed off to a national chain counter.

How this started

Big Bass Signs was founded by two fishing buddies who saw a need for quick, competent sign work you could actually call on. Not a 17-year-old at a national chain. A professional who grew up here, lives here, and will take care of you here.

Born in Sammamish, raised in Woodinville, based in Lynnwood. One-off installs about 90–120 minutes out. Multi-site work anywhere in Washington.

An easy process

Email the job. Rough artwork gets cleaned up. Current city and county posting specs get checked so the board matches what is required now. AI helps with both of those — the specs and the file — so you are not stuck redrawing a logo or hunting a PDF from 2019.

Licensed, bonded, and insured. License BIGBABS743C4. Printers and the laminator are on the equipment page. Cities are on service area.
