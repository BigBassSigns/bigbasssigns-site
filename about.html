About

One shop. Posts in the ground and vinyl on the table.

Big Bass Signs is a Lynnwood print-and-install shop. The name is a fishing joke. The work is land-use boards, construction signs, banners, and the specialty stickers people drive here for.

Chris Strandin. Shop on 206th Place SW. Two Roland VG3 printers and a Seal 54E laminator. The truck carries posts, a shovel, and finished boards — not a sales deck.

Born in Sammamish, grew up in Woodinville, lives in Lynnwood. That is why those cities, plus Everett and Mill Creek, get real pages instead of a keyword dump.

Close partners help on jobs. This site is the shop, not a staff directory.
