Lynnwood sign company

A Lynnwood sign company that also sets the posts.

Big Bass Signs prints land use signs, construction signs, banners, and lettering — then installs them so your crew stays on their own work.

Chris Strandin owns the shop. Two Roland VG3 printers and a Seal 54E laminator. The truck carries finished boards and post-hole tools.

Most of the work is the regulated board: public notice, land use signs, SWPPP signs, and construction site signs. Banners and lettering run next to that.

Licensed, bonded, and insured. License BIGBABS743C4. COI on request. Amounts stay on the certificate.
