Construction & land

Construction signs and land use boards for people who already have a job to run.

Land use action signs, public notice boards, SWPPP contact signs, OSHA headers, project identification, and grant closeout boards. Printed in Lynnwood. Posted on site. Pulled when the planner or GC says they come down.

Who this page is for

Developers, land-use planners, civil engineers, general contractors, and supers. You already know the notice letter landed. You do not want to become a sign vendor for two weeks. Forward the package. We match the board to the jurisdiction, set the posts, and send the photo.

The same visit can cover a SWPPP contact board, a project ID 4×8, and mesh on the fence if the dates line up. That is usually cheaper than three shops guessing three specs.

What we print and install

  • Land use signs and public notice boards
  • SWPPP signs and tree-protection signs
  • OSHA and WA L&I safety headers
  • Construction site signs and project boards
  • Grant and agency funded project signs
  • Mesh banners for chain-link perimeters

4×4 and 4×8 faces are the core. Coroplast for short-run temp work. MDO and exterior plywood when the board has to last the comment period or the build.

From entitlement through occupancy

A lot of sites need more than one board over the life of the job. The public notice goes up first. SWPPP and safety go up with the trailer. Project ID stays until closeout. Grant acknowledgment sometimes replaces all of that. See the stage map if you are ordering for a project that will live on the site for a year.

Email the address, the due date, and the PDF. Install is the default on boards that have to stand in the ground. Board-only is fine if your crew is already on site with a post-hole digger.

Construction signs this shop actually builds

Land use signs and public notice boards. SWPPP contact signs. Tree-protection and silt-fence marking. OSHA and L&I headers. Project identification 4×4 and 4×8 boards. Grant and agency funded project signs. Mesh banners on chain link when the site needs a branded face that does not catch wind like a sail.

This is print-and-install work, not a catalog of stock “construction ahead” paddles. If you need MUTCD traffic control, rentals, or flagging, that is a different vendor. If you need the board the planner, inspector, or owner’s rep is asking for, that is this shop.

Turnaround and radius

Temp coroplast can print the same day when the file is clean before noon. Finished MDO and plywood boards typically leave in two to three days. One-off posts inside about 90–120 minutes of Lynnwood. Multi-site packages go anywhere in Washington.

Licensed, bonded, and insured. COI on request for the GC or owner. Email Sales@BigBassSigns.com the site address, jurisdiction, due date, and the notice PDF.
