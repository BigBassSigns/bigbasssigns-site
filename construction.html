Land-use boards, public notice, SWPPP contact, OSHA headers, and 4×4 / 4×8 project signs. The posting rules change by city and county. You should not be reading King County Permitting and Snohomish County PDS at 9 p.m. to figure out the header.

Forward the notice or the completeness letter. We match the packet, print the board, and put it in the ground when install is part of the job.

What we print and post

  • Land use signs and public notice boards
  • SWPPP / SW3P contact signs and tree protection
  • OSHA and construction safety signs
  • 4×4 and 4×8 project boards — print, install, remove
  • Grant and agency funded project signs
  • Mesh banners for construction fence

Materials that last on a jobsite

Coroplast for short clocks. MDO and corrugated plastic for boards that have to stay through weather. Vinyl faces, lamination, mesh banners that do not catch wind like a sail. We print up to 54 inches on the printers; public banner sizes are the 4-ft line on the banner page.

Turnaround

Temporary boards same day when the file is clean. Permanent boards in two to three days. Install from the truck inside about 90–120 minutes of Lynnwood. Multi-site work goes anywhere in Washington.

Email the address, the jurisdiction, the due date, size or “match the packet,” and install yes or no.
