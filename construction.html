Construction & land

The board in the ground is the product.

Land use signs, public notice signs, SWPPP boards, OSHA / WA L&I, and project ID — printed in Lynnwood and set on posts so your carpenter stays on the building.

Email the packet

Land use & public notice

Built to the county or city packet: King 4×4, Snohomish 4×8 or 2×3, Island and Whatcom mount-and-post when they hand you the notice.

Project boards & install

4×4 and 4×8 MDO or plywood, 4×4 posts, photo for the affidavit, take-down when the planner says pull it.

SWPPP & tree protection

Stormwater contact boards and tree-save fencing signs that stay readable in rain.

OSHA / WA L&I

Danger, Warning, hard-hat, and keep-out headers. Labor-law posters in a weatherproof board if you need that too.

Grant & agency

DOT, HUD, Ecology, and municipal acknowledgment boards from the spec sheet — not a guess.

Entitlement to occupancy

Notice board first. Safety and SWPPP next. Directional and leasing last.

Who sends the email

Sometimes the civil PE. Sometimes the developer’s entitlement person. Sometimes the superintendent who just got the completeness letter. The buck moves. The board still has to match the file number, sit on the midpoint of frontage, and stay up for the comment period.

We pull the current notice packet for that jurisdiction, set type or print your layout, laminate or vinyl-face it for Western Washington weather, and install. You get the photo for the affidavit.
