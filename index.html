Construction & land

Real estate & business

Yard signs, banners, fence mesh, storefront vinyl, window film, A-frames. Built for five to seven seasons of PNW weather, not a weekend open house only.

Vehicles & stickers

Door kits, contour-cut decals, boat-carpet graphics, and domed boat letters. Full wraps are not the lead product. Lettering that reads at 30 feet is.

What a quote needs

Email is the fastest way in. Printers are running and the phone is often in a pocket on a post hole. Send Sales@BigBassSigns.com the job address and jurisdiction, due date, size, whether we install, and artwork or “you set the type.” Site photos help. They are not required.

Temp boards can go the same day when the packet is clean. Permanent boards typically ship or post in two to three days. Standard one-off radius is about 90–120 minutes from Lynnwood. Multi-site work goes farther.
